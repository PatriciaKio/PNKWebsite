Sustainability and the Built Environment · School of Architecture · University of Florida

Designing out waste, one building product at a time

I research and teach how circular economy principles, immersive virtual reality, and artificial intelligence can help future architects, engineers, and builders turn waste into valuable building materials.

Explore my research

Research focus

  • Circular economy education

    Project-based, constructivist courses where students transform non-hazardous waste into proof-of-concept circular building products.

  • Immersive design with BIM + VR

    Workflows that bring Building Information Modeling into virtual reality so students can review, critique, and refine designs at full scale.

  • Data-driven decision tools

    Material circularity metrics, U.S. EPA WARM analysis, and the Circular Building Material Avoidance Calculator for procurement and planning.

Highlights from recent work

  • 0.75 – 1.0 Material Circularity Indicator scores achieved by three student-led case studies
  • 4.92 / 5 Average graduate-student course rating, Fall 2025
  • 2 → 13 Course enrollment growth from Fall 2024 to Fall 2025
  • ~30 minutes VR design-review sessions that helped students self-critique their buildings
