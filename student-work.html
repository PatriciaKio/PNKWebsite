Student work

Circular building products, prototypes, and design projects created by students. Work is shared with each student's written permission, in line with FERPA.

Featured case studies

These three projects are described in the 2026 Frontiers in Sustainability article.

  • Fall 2024 · MCI 1.0

    Footsteps

    Discarded sneaker soles grown together with mycelium and cast in 3D-printed formwork to make modular garden pavement. Prototypes were weathered outdoors over summer 2025 and tested with a thermal imaging camera.

    Student name(s): to be added with permission

  • Fall 2025 · MCI 0.90

    Lotus-weave shingle panel

    A three-layer roofing shingle: lotus-textured waste tire rubber on top, a spiral recycled-wire skeleton in the middle, and a pressed cardboard and wood-fiber mat below. Embedded IoT sensors track moisture, temperature, UV, and strain.

    Student name(s): to be added with permission

  • Fall 2025 · MCI 0.75

    Plastic-bottle green wall

    Sand-filled PET bottles set in local earth form a vegetated wall with thermal mass and evaporative cooling. Removable IoT modules monitor temperature, moisture, and cracking.

    Student name(s): to be added with permission
