Accessibility statement

I want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website and its research materials.

Standards

This site aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and follows WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. This is the standard referenced by:

  • the U.S. Department of Justice rule under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act for state and local government entities, including Florida's public universities;
  • Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, which Florida adopts for state information technology through section 282.603, Florida Statutes;
  • University of Florida digital accessibility policy for web content.

What this site does

  • A “Skip to main content” link and clear page landmarks (header, navigation, main, footer).
  • Full keyboard access, with a visible focus outline on every link and button.
  • Text and controls meet colour-contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1, in both light and dark modes; information is never conveyed by colour alone.
  • Pages reflow on screens as narrow as 320 pixels and at up to 400% zoom without sideways scrolling.
  • Headings follow a logical order; data tables use captions and header cells.
  • All images, posters, and charts have text alternatives; posters include a full text version.
  • Clickable targets are at least 24 by 24 pixels, and main navigation links are at least 44 pixels tall.
  • Motion is limited and turned off for people who set “reduce motion” on their device.
  • Every page works without JavaScript.

Documents, images, and video

Before a PDF, poster, or student project is posted, it is checked to make sure that:

  • PDFs are tagged, have a document title and language, a logical reading order, and alternative text for figures (checked with Adobe Acrobat's Accessibility Checker or PAC);
  • images have meaningful alternative text, and complex figures have a longer description;
  • videos have accurate captions, and audio has a transcript.

Known limitations

  • Some links go to publisher websites that are not managed by this site and may have their own accessibility barriers.
  • Older conference PDFs may not yet be fully tagged. An accessible version is available on request.

Feedback and alternative formats

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need a document in another format (such as accessible PDF, Word, plain text, or large print), please contact me. Tell me the page address and the problem you found. I aim to respond within 5 business days.

University of Florida students who need academic accommodations can also contact the UF Disability Resource Center.
